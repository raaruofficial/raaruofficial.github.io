The Problem

ORE miners compete round after round, but the information that decides outcomes — who is winning, how exposure is concentrated, what changed, and what looks anomalous — is scattered across raw on-chain activity.

Without a dedicated intelligence layer, players are mining blind while better-informed wallets compound their edge.

The Product

🧭 Miner Dossiers

Profiles on leading mining wallets — history, patterns, and exposure in one readable view.

▶️ Round Replays

Cinematic replays of ORE rounds, so players can study how a round actually unfolded.

🏆 Leaderboards

A top-150 leaderboard with the context behind the ranking — not just names and numbers.

📡 Anomaly Radar

ANOMALY radar over ORE mining rounds, surfacing unusual activity worth a second look.

How It Works

  • Weekly, byte-frozen data drops. Each release is a fixed snapshot — so “this week’s drop” honestly means this week’s data, identical for every user.
  • Real coverage. Snapshot coverage includes rounds 455079–456632 and grows drop by drop; the current leaderboard covers 150 leaders.
  • Self-contained on device. Content ships inside the app. Payments in SOL / USDC on Solana are verified on-device — the only network call a sale requires is verifying its own settlement.
