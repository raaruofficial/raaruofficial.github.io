The Problem

Every ORE round is a contest of information. Who is deploying, where exposure is concentrating, what just changed, what doesn’t look right — the answers decide who wins, and they’re buried in raw on-chain activity no human can read at speed. Serious miners know they’re flying blind. They just have no alternative.

How It Works

  • Weekly drops, byte-frozen. Every release is a fixed snapshot — rounds 455079–456632 and counting — identical for every user, so the intelligence you paid for is the intelligence you get.
  • Self-contained on the Seeker. Content ships inside the app and lives on the device. No account, no tracking, no server holding your edge hostage.
  • Paid in SOL / USDC, verified on-device. The only network call a sale ever makes is verifying its own settlement.
