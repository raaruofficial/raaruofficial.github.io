HoloSeekr

A card vault for Pokémon & One Piece collectors, built for the Solana Seeker and published through the Solana dApp Store by Raaru.

WHAT IT DOES

  • The Vault — record the physical cards you own: graded slabs by certificate number, cards picked from the catalog, or a whole collection imported from a CSV export (Collectr, TCGplayer, eBay). What you paid and when is yours to record alongside each card.
  • Real sold prices — for cards in your vault, HoloSeekr shows what the same card actually sold for recently, drawn from completed eBay sales through a licensed data provider. Every figure carries its source and date. When there are not enough recent sales to stand behind a number, the app says so instead of guessing.
  • The Showcase — connect your wallet and HoloSeekr reads the public chain to show the tokenized cards it holds, matched against a curated set of recognized collections. Your wallet signs the connection; your keys never leave your wallet app.
  • The Value view — one total for what your collection is worth, with an honest coverage line showing how many of your cards have a current valuation behind them.

HONEST SCOPE — v0.1

HoloSeekr is free. There is nothing to buy in the app and no payment surface of any kind.

Pokémon catalog coverage is the launch strength. One Piece support is in early rollout — vault entries work, but catalog and price coverage for One Piece cards is still limited and growing.

The Showcase recognizes a curated set of tokenized-card collections, and that set grows through publisher partnerships. A wallet holding only collections outside the recognized set will show an empty Showcase — that is the honest answer, not an error.

HoloSeekr is not a marketplace: it never takes custody of cards or money, and it does not grade cards or certify authenticity. Prices are records of past sales, not a promise of what a card will sell for.

PRIVACY

HoloSeekr has no ads, no analytics trackers, and no third-party data SDKs. We do not sell user data — there is no data business behind this app.

To work, the app talks to the HoloSeekr service, and the service receives and stores: the vault entries you create (card, grade, certificate number, and any notes or purchase details you record), the CSV files you choose to import, your catalog and price searches, and — only when you run a Showcase scan — your wallet's public address, which the service uses to look up that wallet's public on-chain assets. On-chain data is public by nature; your vault entries are not published anywhere.

Wallet connection uses the standard Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol. Approvals happen in your wallet app; HoloSeekr never sees or stores private keys or seed phrases, and it cannot move anything in your wallet.

To have your stored vault data deleted, email raaruofficial+support@gmail.com and we will remove it.
