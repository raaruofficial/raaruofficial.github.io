Seeker Citadel

THE PERMANENT RECORD OF SEEKER LIFE

Seeker Citadel is a community forum for owners of the Seeker device: a permanent, searchable, on-device record of Seeker life, posted in by verified owners. It is published by Raaru through the Solana dApp Store.

An independent community forum for Seeker owners. Not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by Solana or Solana Mobile.

THE BOARDS

Five boards, one identity

The Square — general Seeker talk and introductions, home of the Well, where members leave one-line wishes for the ecosystem.
dApps & Games — dApp Store shipping, reviews, requests.
SKR — staking and rewards. Nothing there is financial advice.
Device & Seed Vault — hardware and firmware: community help, not official support.
Builders — dev talk, Mobile Wallet Adapter, publishing, rejection post-mortems.

Reading is free and needs no wallet. Posting is for Seeker Genesis Token holders: you sign in with your wallet, and the forum checks — on chain, by token group membership, never by name — that your wallet holds a Seeker Genesis Token. Your forum name is your wallet: everything you post is permanently linkable to your on-chain finances, and the app tells you so before you sign in, not after.

HOW IT WORKS

Seeker Citadel is a native app — no web view, no web client. Drafts and cached reading live on your device; the forum host keeps the shared record. There are no payments, no tips, and no held funds in this version. Members can report content, and members can block other members; see the Terms for how moderation works.
