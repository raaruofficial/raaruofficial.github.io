Privacy Policy — Seeker Citadel

Seeker Citadel is published by Raaru. This policy describes what the Seeker Citadel service stores, what it never stores, and how long it keeps what it stores. Last updated 2026-10-30.

An independent community forum for Seeker owners. Not endorsed by, affiliated with, or sponsored by Solana or Solana Mobile.

1. Your wallet is your identity

Seeker Citadel has no accounts, passwords, or email sign-up. When you sign in, you sign a message with your Solana wallet (Sign-In with Solana). Your wallet address is your forum name: posts, replies, reports, and moderation actions are recorded against that address, and everything you do in the forum is permanently linkable to your on-chain finances. The app shows you this disclosure before you sign in. Forum content is a public, permanent record — do not post anything you would not want publicly attributed to your wallet.

2. What the host stores

  • Member record: your wallet address, the date your Seeker Genesis Token eligibility was verified, reputation, moderation standing (strikes, mutes, bans), and — if you claim one — your founding number.
  • Your content: threads, replies, poll votes, reports you file, follows, and blocks.
  • Sessions: when you sign in, the host issues a session token. Only a SHA-256 hash of the token is stored; the token itself is shown to your device once and never stored. Sessions expire 7 days after issue and are not renewed silently.
  • Eligibility proof (internal only): at sign-in the host records the Seeker Genesis Token mint(s) your wallet held, as an internal join key (for example, so one device token cannot claim two founding spots). Mint addresses are never displayed in the app, never shown on any public surface, and never returned by the service to any user or role. Public surfaces show only eligibility state — that a member is a verified holder — never a mint.

3. What the host never stores

  • No IP addresses and no user-agent strings are stored anywhere in the service, and no network identifier is ever joined to a member, a wallet, or a session. Rate limiting is keyed on wallet addresses and member records, not on network identifiers.
  • No private keys, seed phrases, or signing material — signing happens in your wallet on your device; the host only verifies signatures.
  • No payment information. Seeker Citadel has no payments, no checkout, and no held funds.
  • No advertising identifiers, analytics beacons, or cross-app tracking. The app contains no web view.

4. The one external service: the RPC provider

To verify Seeker Genesis Token eligibility at sign-in, the host must ask the Solana blockchain what tokens your wallet holds. That query goes to our RPC provider, Alchemy (a dedicated Seeker Citadel application key), with the public Solana mainnet endpoint (api.mainnet-beta.solana.com) as an automatic failover. The query contains your wallet address — which is public on-chain information — and nothing else about you. The host makes no wallet-keyed chain reads outside sign-in (and, in any future paid version, payment verification). What an RPC provider can see is limited to the queries described here; we do not send them your posts, reports, or session data.

5. Retention

  • Sign-in challenges (the one-time nonces behind sign-in) are deleted within 24 hours of use or expiry.
  • Session records are deleted 7 days after the session expires.
  • Poll ballots are deleted 7 days after a poll closes; the tallies are kept.
  • Forum content is the permanent record described in §1 and is kept unless removed under the moderation rules in the Terms of Use; a removed post's declared outbound link, if any, is deleted at removal or within 12 months, whichever comes first.

6. On your device

The app keeps drafts and a cache of what you have read on your device so the forum opens and reads offline. That data never leaves your device except the posts you choose to publish. Uninstalling the app removes it.

7. Children, changes, contact

Seeker Citadel is for Seeker owners and is not directed at children under 13. If this policy changes materially, the new version will be posted here with a new date before it takes effect. Questions about this policy: raaruofficial+support@gmail.com.
