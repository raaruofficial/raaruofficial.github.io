2. Eligibility and your wallet

Posting is for Seeker Genesis Token holders. Sessions last 7 days; eligibility is re-verified at each new sign-in, so if the token leaves your wallet, posting access ends at your next sign-in. Your forum name is your wallet address. Everything you post is public, permanent, and linkable to your on-chain activity. You are responsible for your wallet and for everything posted under it.

5. Moderation — how it actually works

Seeker Citadel is moderated by a combination of community reporting, automatic thresholds, and an operator surface. Read this section carefully: it describes the moderation that actually ships, and it is the whole of what is promised.

  • Report/flag: any signed-in member can report a thread or reply in the app, with a reason (spam, abuse, misinformation, unlabelled commercial, or other). One report per member per item.
  • Auto-moderation: reports carry weight. When the accumulated weight on an item reaches the published threshold, the item is hidden automatically and its author receives a strike. Strikes escalate automatically: a warning, then a 6-hour mute, then a 48-hour mute, then a ban. Members whose reports prove out gain reputation; members with high reputation report at higher weight and, as curators, can vote to restore hidden content — a quorum of curators restores it.
  • Operator surface: the publisher's operator can review the moderation queue (hidden content with its report aggregates), hide or unhide content, dismiss reports, mute, ban, unban members, and revoke a member's sessions. Operator actions are logged. The public moderation feed shows moderation actions in aggregate, identity-free form — it never names who reported anything. A reported member is told the reason category, never who reported them.
  • Self-help: you don't have to wait for moderation. You can block any member — their threads and replies disappear from your view — and you can follow or unfollow members at any time. Blocking is immediate and entirely yours.
  • No human review is promised. Reports are acted on by the automatic system and the operator surface described above; no term here promises that a human will review any particular report, post, or appeal, and no response time is promised for reports, appeals, or support messages. If you believe content was wrongly hidden, you may write to the support address in §9; a wrongful classification carries no standing strike once reversed.
